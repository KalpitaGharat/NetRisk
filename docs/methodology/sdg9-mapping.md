# Alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goal 9

## 1. Goal Overview
**Sustainable Development Goal 9 (SDG 9)**: *Build resilient infrastructure, promote inclusive and sustainable industrialization, and foster innovation.*

As modern critical industrial infrastructure (power grids, banking backbones, telecommunication switching, transportation routing, and healthcare facilities) transitions to digital and automated controls, software systems become the foundational infrastructure of society.

---

## 2. Core Pillars of NETRISK Alignment

NETRISK directly aligns with SDG 9 through five concrete technical contributions:

### 1. Resilient Digital Infrastructure (Target 9.1)
Digital networks are critical infrastructure. Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) serve as the primary defensive shield preventing unauthorized infiltration, ransomware, and denial-of-service sabotage. By ensuring that AI-based IDS systems are built using rigorous, risk-tailored lifecycle models (such as the V-Model or DevSecOps), NETRISK minimizes systemic vulnerabilities that could compromise public utility networks and enterprise backbones.

### 2. Cybersecurity-Aware Software Engineering (Target 9.4)
Traditional SDLC selection often ignores cyber-risk dimensions, treating security as an afterthought tacked on at deployment. NETRISK embeds security criticality (25%) and threat volatility (15%) as primary mathematical determinants of the development lifecycle, ensuring security practices are shifted left into architectural planning.

### 3. Responsible AI Adoption (Target 9.5)
Integrating Machine Learning and Deep Learning into security operations introduces novel challenges: adversarial evasion attacks, data poisoning, model drift, and high false-alarm rates. NETRISK incorporates AI/ML complexity (20%) to steer high-complexity projects toward risk-driven iterative paradigms (e.g., the Spiral Model), mandating continuous model calibration and validation.

### 4. Technology-Driven Risk Management (Target 9.b)
Rather than relying on informal intuitions or rigid organizational biases, NETRISK provides an objective, explainable decision-support platform. This elevates engineering governance by making software risk factors quantifiable, auditable, and traceable.

### 5. Reliable Software Development Practices (Target 9.c)
NETRISK dynamically synthesizes concrete software engineering practices—such as failure-impact analysis, bidirectional compliance matrices, and continuous integration security gates—ensuring teams maintain disciplined engineering standards across the product lifecycle.
